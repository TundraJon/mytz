1 Open an image

How this step works

Tap Open image and choose a picture from Photos, Files or Downloads. Any PNG, JPG or WebP works. The image never leaves your phone.

Tip: use the largest, cleanest version of your artwork you have. The tool shrinks it down to 64×64 (and optionally 128×128), so more source detail means a sharper result.

2 Choose the square

How this step works

The bright box is the part of your image that becomes the tile. It is always a perfect square. Everything outside the box is dimmed and thrown away.

  • Move: drag inside the box with one finger.
  • Resize: drag one of the four white corner squares.
  • Draw a new box: drag on the dimmed area.
  • Zoom / pan the picture: pinch with two fingers (dragging with two fingers pans). Or use the Fit / 100% / Zoom to box buttons.
  • Exact edges: zoom in on the box's edge, then use the arrow pad (tap or hold). Set the pad to 1 px for precision or 10 px for speed. You can also type exact X, Y and Size numbers.

The label on the picture shows the box position and size in source pixels. Snap ×64 sets the size to a clean multiple of 64, which gives the sharpest shrink.

Downscale method: Smooth blends pixels (best for detailed art); Crisp picks single pixels (best for pixel art).

Page won't scroll? Touching the picture moves the box, so scroll by touching the dark space outside it.

3 Make the background transparent

How this step works
  1. Tap Pick color, then touch and hold on the Original picture below. A magnifier appears above your finger. Slide to the background pixel you want gone and lift to add it. (Tap Pick color again for another.)
  2. Quick option: Pick 4 corners grabs the background color from all four corners.
  3. Adjust Shades darker / lighter. Example: background is off-white and you want to also remove colors up to 3 shades away in either direction, so set both to 3. Higher values remove more.
  4. Watch the Result picture and the little previews in the bar at the bottom of the screen. The result is shown on a dark background so leftover fringe is easy to spot.

Remove where: Connected to the edges only removes background touching the outside of the picture, so matching colors inside your icon (white eyes, highlights) survive. Everywhere removes every matching pixel.

Clean edge color fixes the pale halo: edge pixels are usually a blend of your art and the old background, so their color is replaced with the nearby art color. Raise it if a light fringe shows on dark. Lower it (or use 0) if thin outlines start disappearing.

Soft edge fades pixels that are almost, but not quite, background. Shrink edge eats a thin layer off the outline.

The removal is done on the full-size crop before shrinking, so edges come out naturally smooth instead of stair-stepped.

4 Check the preview

How this step works

These show your finished 64×64 tile on a light background, a dark background and a checkerboard (checkerboard = the transparent parts). This is how it will look in light and dark mode in your app.

  • Zoom slider: enlarge the tile. Scroll sideways inside a picture if it is bigger than the screen.
  • Pixel grid: shows each real tile pixel.
  • Change the light / dark colors to match your app's actual backgrounds.
  • Actual size is the real 64×64 at 1×. The dark version there is what to judge for jagged edges.

Not happy? Go back to step 3 and adjust. Nothing is saved until step 5.

5 Save your PNG

How this step works
  • Tick the size(s) you want: 64×64 (default), 128×128, or both. The 128 version is made straight from your full-size crop, not stretched from the 64.
  • Type a file name. The size is added automatically, e.g. sword_64.png and sword_128.png. Turn on Auto-number to get sword_001_64.png, then sword_002_64.png on the next save, so files never overwrite each other.
  • Tap Save PNG (bottom bar). Files go to your phone's Downloads folder. If Chrome asks to allow multiple downloads, tap Allow.
  • Share sends the file(s) to another app (Drive, Messages, email…) without saving first.

PNG keeps the transparent background, which is what lets one file work in both light and dark mode.

Tile Maker 64 — how to use it

This tool turns any picture into a small, square, transparent-background PNG tile (64×64, 128×128 or both) that works on light and dark backgrounds.

Quick start

  1. Open image — pick a picture.
  2. Choose the square — drag the box (corners resize it) around the part you want.
  3. Make the background transparent — Pick color, touch-and-hold the background in the Original picture, lift. Adjust the shade sliders until it looks right.
  4. Check the preview on light and dark.
  5. Save PNG from the bottom bar.

Getting around

  • Every step has a blue “How this step works” box. Tap it to collapse or expand.
  • The bar at the bottom always shows a live mini preview on light and dark, plus Save and Share.
  • Undo / Redo (top) cover the box position, colors and all sliders.

Common problems

  • Pale/light fringe around the icon on dark: raise Clean edge color to 2, and/or set Shrink edge to 1. Check the dark preview.
  • Holes appear inside my icon: set Remove where to “Connected to the edges”, or lower the shade sliders.
  • Some background is left: raise the shade sliders a little, pick another background color (add several), or try Pick 4 corners.
  • Thin outlines vanished: lower Clean edge color or Shrink edge to 0.
  • Edges look chunky: use Smooth downscale, and try Snap ×64 for the crop size.
  • Can't find the saved file: it is in your phone's Downloads folder (Files app).
  • The page won't scroll: touching the picture in step 2 moves the box. Scroll from the space outside it.
  • Very large photos: the background is removed on a version of the crop capped at 2048 px, which is more than enough for a tile. The live preview uses a lighter version while you drag sliders, then refines itself a moment after you stop.
